Águas de Iara

Developer Curipira Studios Release DEC 2026 ★★★★★

The Legend Awakens

Dive into the mesmerizing depths of the Amazon River in Águas de Iara, a third-person action-adventure inspired by the rich tapestry of Brazilian folklore. You play as a traveler chosen by the Lady of the Waters herself, tasked with purifying the sacred tributaries from an encroaching industrial rot.

Master the "Fluid Flow" combat system, where your movements mirror the current of the river. Navigate lush submerged ruins, interact with legendary entities like the Boto and the Boitatá, and discover the hidden secrets of the forest floor.

Hydro-Dynamic Combat

Harness water-based abilities to manipulate the battlefield and outmaneuver enemies.

Mythical Ecosystem

Interact with AI-driven creatures from Tupi-Guarani lore in a living, breathing world.
